Models: Mini Characters (character-male-a), Prototype Kit (coin pickup mesh), Platformer Kit (crate), City Kit Commercial (building and awning), City Kit Roads (barrier), Food Kit (bottle, bowl and barrel). Nature Kit (palm tree).

The in-game collectible currency remains Zarad-branded. No Subway Surfers proprietary Unity binaries, characters, textures, or extracted assets are included.


The Kenney coin mesh is used for the in-game currency pickup, recoloured in the WebGL renderer with Zarad gold and a star detail. The procedural Zarad runner, Moroccan market environment, rails, trains, obstacles, and power-up effects are original site-side adaptations.
