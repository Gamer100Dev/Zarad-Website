Models: Mini Characters (character-male-a), Prototype Kit (gold coin pickup), Platformer Kit (crate), City Kit Commercial (building and awning), City Kit Roads (barrier), Food Kit (bottle, bowl and barrel), Nature Kit (palm tree).

The live coin pickup uses the CC0 Kenney coin model, recoloured Zarad gold with an original Zarad centre detail. The player is an original Zarad-styled articulated character. The Moroccan souk uses the project's warm palette and original market staging, with Kenney awnings, spice bowls, barrels, crates, barriers and palms. No Subway Surfers proprietary Unity binaries, characters, textures or extracted assets are included.
